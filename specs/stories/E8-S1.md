## Description
Render the secondary feature: a visual Streamlit grid of product cards fetched from the catalog endpoint, each showing image, name, brand, category, price, and stock status.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. On load, the app fetches `GET /api/products` and renders the products as a grid of cards.
2. Each card displays image, name, brand, category, price (₹), and stock status.
3. A product with `stock=0` visibly shows an "out of stock" indicator.
4. When the catalog endpoint is unavailable, the UI shows the BRD-style friendly message rather than a stack trace.
